[tutorial_progressive_speed_title]
Progressive Speed
---
[tutorial_step_intro]
This breathing practice is based on the Wim Hof Method.

It can be powerful. Use it with care.

Practice sitting or lying down.
Never use it while driving,
standing, or in water.
---
[tutorial_step_method]
Simply follow 4 steps:

1. Breathe rhythmically.
2. Exhale and hold.
3. Inhale deeply and hold.
4. Exhale and repeat.

Use the wrench icon %i to adjust rounds, breaths, speed, and pauses.
---
[tutorial_step_breathe]
Fill your lungs fully, then let the breath flow out.

Use this slider to set the pace of the breathing circle.

Find a rhythm that feels natural for you.
---
[tutorial_step_progressive_speed]
Progressive speed makes the first round easier to enter.

When it is on, the first round starts slower, then gradually increases to your selected speed.
---
[tutorial_step_exhale_hold]
Exhale normally and hold.

The center number keeps counting. Circle mode adds layered progress lines.

Release when your body asks for air.
---
[tutorial_step_inhale_hold]
Inhale fully and hold for about 15 seconds.

Then exhale and begin the next round.
Over time, each round may feel deeper.
