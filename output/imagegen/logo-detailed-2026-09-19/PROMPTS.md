The cloud is a soft cushion of light, aqua, lavender and pale pink with beautifully modeled volume.
Composition for Android: create the visible square face of an app icon, designed to be cropped by a circle or rounded square. All of the person and the important cloud silhouette must fit comfortably inside an imaginary circle occupying the central 84 percent of the square; leave clear breathing room around head and cloud. Person should be visually prominent, cloud supporting rather than overwhelming. Image extends to the four edges.
Background: opaque edge-to-edge soft sky-blue atmosphere, subtly lighter behind the figure, smoothly shifting into deeper cool blue toward the outer corners. Background must be painted, not transparent. No blank white field, no checkerboard.
Palette: original sky blue, aqua, lavender, pale blush, warm peach skin, richer blue clothing; add contrast through coordinated deep blue shadows and warm light. Favor vibrant but harmonious colors.
Lighting: gentle directional warm light, convincing depth and soft contact shadows where body rests on cloud. A serene, alive, inviting character.
Constraints: single finished icon artwork only; no text, labels, letters, watermarks, mockup phone, border, rounded-square frame, visible circle outline, extruded app tile, medallion, extra people, extra objects, religious symbols, flowers or clutter. No featureless mannequin, stick figure, silhouette, corporate flat vector, line logo or generic minimal glyph. Detail must be organized into clear readable masses at 48dp.
