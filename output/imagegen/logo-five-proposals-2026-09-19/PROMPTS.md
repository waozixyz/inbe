# Five detailed Inbe logo proposals

Created with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-19.

Design brief: preserve the original meditator-on-cloud identity; add graphic detail and personality while avoiding both generic flat symbols and realistic character illustrations. Android launcher readability remains the main application.

## 01-ink-and-cloud.png — Ink and cloud

Use case: logo-brand.
Deliverable: ONE detailed, beautiful pictorial LOGO proposal for the Inbe Android app. Square 1024x1024, a single isolated emblem centered on a perfectly uniform sky-blue (#87CEEB) background.
Reference image 1: the original Inbe logo. Retain its identity: a peach-colored meditator in blue, seated cross-legged on an aqua/lavender/pale-pink cloud.
User feedback is decisive: the last proposals became realistic character illustrations and were rejected. Earlier minimal generic symbols were also rejected. This must be a GRAPHIC LOGO with thoughtful detail and personality.
Visual construction: clearly stylized and deliberately drawn. Crisp silhouettes, strong color separation, graceful interior contours, a few expressive fabric folds and designed cloud curls. Show depth with two or three sharply bounded color shapes per material. Detail comes from graphic craftsmanship, not realistic rendering.
Human: symbolic, gender-neutral, elegant compact proportions. Small simple peach head; if eyes appear use just two tiny curved marks. No rendered nose, lips, ears, muscles, breasts, hair, realistic anatomy or expressive portrait. The figure's flowing pose and the cloud's rhythm provide its life.
Palette derived from original: warm peach, rich blue and teal, icy aqua, lavender and a small touch of pink; dark indigo for definition, a restrained light accent. Maintain clear contrast on the background.
Android composition: entire meaningful emblem comfortably inside an imaginary circle occupying the central 84 percent of the square. Approximately 12 percent clear margin. Compact and optically centered. Head, knees and cloud should all remain readable at 48dp.
Strict exclusions: no photorealism, no 3D, no cinematic lighting, no digital painting, no soft airbrushed shading, no realistic skin, no scenic sky, no scenery or background clouds. No lifeless solid pictogram or stick figure. No text, lettering, wordmarks, slogans, watermark, phone mockup, presentation sheet, extra symbols, lotus flower, sun, stars or decorations unrelated to the original. One standalone finished logo, not a brand board. Background is a single solid color, all detail stays inside the emblem.
Specific proposal 1 — Ink and cloud. A polished two-dimensional illustrated emblem, using confident dark-indigo tapered outlines, fluid blue clothing, small restrained cel-shadow planes, and a compact beautifully curled cloud. Keep the original recognizable upright cross-legged silhouette but redraw it with much more elegant proportions. Use three purposeful curved folds in the garment, neatly overlapping crossed legs, peach hands suggested as small wedge shapes, and a modest peach head with two discreet closed-eye arcs. The cloud is a low, cohesive aqua cushion with two ornamental curls and a lavender underside, delicately outlined with subtle pink highlights. High-quality editorial logo drawing, sophisticated and inviting. Strong unified outer silhouette, no large cartoon head, no oversized fluffy cloud.
