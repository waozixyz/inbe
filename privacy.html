Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Inner Breeze

Inner Breeze is a free, open-source guided breathing meditation app based on the Wim Hof method.

Data Collection

Inner Breeze does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No user accounts or authentication
  • No personal information collection
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No network requests or data transmission
  • No third-party services

Local Data Storage

The app stores session history locally on your device:

  • Breathing session timing data (duration of each breath round)
  • Unlimited session history
  • Data remains on your device only
  • Clear app data to delete all stored sessions

Data Deletion

To delete your session data: Clear the app's data through your device's settings.

Permissions

Inner Breeze requires no special permissions on Android or other platforms.

Children's Privacy

Inner Breeze is suitable for all ages. Since no personal data is collected, it is COPPA compliant.
